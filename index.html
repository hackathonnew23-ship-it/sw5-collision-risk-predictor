Conjunction Desk: will this close approach turn dangerous?

A model learns from the conjunction data messages (CDMs) that arrive at least a set number of days before closest approach, predicts whether the final collision risk will be dangerous, and recommends manoeuvre or no manoeuvre with a confidence level. Everything below runs live in your browser on simulated CDM streams, so change a setting and retrain to see the trade-offs move.
